# No-Credit Rental Application Packet

## Cover Note
I am an international student at [school/program] seeking a lease from [start] to [end]. I do not yet have an established U.S. credit history. I can provide the following documentation to support my application: [proof of funds / scholarship / assistantship / U.S. bank / guarantor / prior landlord reference]. Please let me know which alternatives your property accepts for applicants without U.S. credit history.

## Documents Checklist
- [ ] Passport/visa only through verified application channel
- [ ] I-20/DS-2019 if requested
- [ ] Enrollment/admission verification
- [ ] U.S. bank statement / proof of funds
- [ ] Scholarship/assistantship/offer letter
- [ ] Previous landlord reference
- [ ] Guarantor documents if applicable
- [ ] Written quote of deposit/fees

## Questions
- Do you require a Social Security Number, or can you process an international applicant without one?
- What alternative financial documents do you accept?
- Is a guarantor mandatory? What qualifications must they meet?
- Is any increased deposit refundable?
- What is the total non-refundable amount if I apply and am approved?
