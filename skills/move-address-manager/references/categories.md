# Address-holder coverage checklist

Use this as a coverage audit, not as proof that the user has every account.

## Government and identity

- postal forwarding and mail holds
- driver's license or state ID; vehicle registration and title
- voter registration
- federal, state, and local tax agencies
- immigration records and pending applications, when applicable
- Social Security or public benefits, when applicable
- passports, trusted-traveler programs, licenses, permits, and court records when applicable

## Work and money

- employer HR, payroll, W-2 delivery, benefits, and equity portals
- retirement plans, pensions, HSA/FSA, stock plans, and transfer agents
- banks, credit unions, credit cards, loans, mortgage/servicer, and safe-deposit records
- brokerage, crypto, payment, tax-preparation, accountant, and insurance accounts
- credit bureaus only when a direct address record or fraud concern applies

## Insurance and health

- auto, renters/homeowners, umbrella, life, disability, travel, and pet insurance
- health, dental, vision, pharmacy, medical providers, and health portals
- vehicle garaging address; do not assume it equals mailing address

## Home and daily life

- landlord, property manager, HOA, mortgage/escrow, county property records, and homestead records
- electricity, gas, water, trash, internet, mobile, alarm, and home services
- schools, childcare, university/alumni, veterinarian, pet microchip registry, and emergency contacts
- attorneys, accountants, professional associations, and licenses

## Shopping and memberships

- major retailers, delivery apps, marketplaces, digital wallets, device accounts, pharmacies, meal kits, and recurring shipments
- gyms, clubs, storage, parking/toll tags, roadside assistance, and transit accounts
- magazines, newspapers, charities, churches, loyalty programs, and subscription boxes
- friends and family who regularly send mail

## Business records

- secretary of state or equivalent entity record
- principal office, business mailing, and registered-agent address as distinct fields
- IRS and state tax accounts, payroll providers, sales-tax permits, and local licenses
- banks, cards, insurance, vendors, customers, invoices, contracts, website, and domain registration
- Google Business Profile and public directories when the business location or service area changes

For each applicable government or business item, verify the current procedure and deadline from the responsible official authority at execution time.
